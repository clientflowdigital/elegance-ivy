Elegance Ivy bags award at the Africa CEO Summit

September 6, 2024

We are thrilled to share that Ivy, the CEO of Elegance Ivy Construction and Interior Design in Zimbabwe, has been honoured with two prestigious awards in Africa!

It was also a privilege to be a panelist at the event in Nigeria, sharing insights and expertise with industry peers. Our team’s commitment to excellence was recognised at the Pan African Youth Leadership Foundation Award ceremonies held in Nigeria and the African Women Achievers awards held in Rwanda, highlighting our dedication to quality and innovation in the construction and interior design industry.

Thank you to our clients and partners for your continued support and trust in our work. Thank you, Zimbabwe.
