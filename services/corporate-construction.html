Corporate Construction

We are a contractor of choice for corporate and institutional construction projects in Zimbabwe.

Scope

We provide construction services for commercial and institutional properties, as well as corporate interior designing for offices and business premises.

Our clients include Econet Wireless, CV People, Schweppes, GBI Business Hub and Aurex Jewellery, and our institutional work includes Karanda Hospital and the Bindura Labour Ward.

Every engagement is run as a one-off: bespoke, tailor-made and delivered with our trademark attention to detail from the conceptual stages to the finished product and beyond.
